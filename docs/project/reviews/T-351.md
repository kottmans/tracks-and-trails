### Implementation assessment

The correction consistently distinguishes a job's position in `_rows` from its position in
the table's `_visible` list. Status, progress and busy-tick signal paths now construct their
indices from visible positions. `_whole_row` reads the selector directly from that job's
`_row_data`, so the spoken format cannot accidentally come from a playlist member above it.

An independent AST comparison confirms that extracting `_row_data` preserves the former
job-row role dispatch exactly, apart from replacing `index.column()` with the column parameter.
The remaining `_index_of` reads look up `_rows` by job id; none constructs a table index.
The other model-built signal indices use `rowCount()` or the new visible-position span.
The change preserves the indexed status lookup, coalesced progress repaint, ending-state
message retirement and existing no-playlist role behavior.

The implementation's stated group rule is consistent with the header's roles. A member's
status affects the header's chip, segments, detail, verbs and available preset actions, so
the header is signalled even when the member is hidden. With the group open, the status span
covers the header and member without reaching an unrelated row. Byte progress and busy
animation belong to drawn job rows; a collapsed header has neither. Hidden progress remains
stored, and a hidden processing member keeps the timer running for when its group opens.
This satisfies the task's required ruling and `REQ-014`/`NFR-005` without changing UX-005's
grouping or format-inheritance behavior.
