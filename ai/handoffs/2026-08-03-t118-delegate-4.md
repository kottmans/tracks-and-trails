## The finding, and why your trace was the whole answer

`_shown` existed and **nothing used it**. `rowCount()`, `data()`, `setData()` and the dialog's
`_row_at` all read `Staging.visible` — the tuple reconciliation had already mutated. So "commit the
editor before the reset, while its index is valid" was true and useless: the index was numerically
valid and had stopped naming the same row.

Your route reproduces exactly: A/B/C, edit to B/C, open old row 1 for B, choose MP3, let the
debounce fire. New tuple is (B, C); old index 1 is C; MP3 lands on C and B stays inherited. Add
durably queues the wrong request for both.

**The correction is what you specified.** Every index resolves through `StagingModel.row_at`, which
reads `_shown`. `commit_open_editor()` runs *before* `_shown` is swapped — one ordering point, and
the whole fix turns on it. Selection restores by row identity. The commit path is re-entrancy-safe,
since `setData` calls `dialog.refresh()`.
