### `T143-R1` — the criterion is amended, and size is deferred rather than dropped

**Ruling: amend to the actual `UX-005` §3 row anatomy, and defer pre-download size.** Taken over the
alternative of authorising the schema work.

The reasoning on the record: **a pasted URL's row has no pre-download size either**, so the wording
was not describing a gap between a playlist entry and a pasted one — which is what this task exists
to close. It described a field the application has for no row at all, and no implementation of
*this* task could have met it. Authorising a `Job` size field plus a migration would have expanded a
two-field write into a schema change landing against an already-signed criterion set.

**The deferral has somewhere to go.** `T-191` — *"A queued row shows no size until it downloads"* —
is filed under `## Proposed`, Phase 4, Low. A criterion narrowed into nowhere is a criterion
deleted, so the entry names the real work: a field on `Job`, a migration, and the part that actually
needs a ruling — what a row says when the number is `filesize_approx` rather than `filesize`, given
`REQ-003` already names the column *"filesize/estimate"* and `T107-R7` made the two distinguishable.

**`ai/TASKS.md` only.** No source, no test. Your note says a focused documentation re-review is
sufficient in that case.

### `T180-R1` — `DAT-007`, accepted

**Ruling: accept the per-database boundary and first-launch adoption**, over a machine-wide cache
with a safely coordinated sweep.

`ai/DECISIONS.md` now carries **`DAT-007` — The thumbnail cache partitions by database, and the
shared one is adopted once**, with the sections this project's decisions carry: context, decision,
why, **rejected**, consequences.

Your framing is transcribed rather than paraphrased, because it is the part the entry had wrong:
**`ARC-006` permits an instance on a different database and does not say where that instance's
cache lives.** The machine-wide alternative was therefore genuinely available and is rejected on the
record — on cost against benefit, since the honest version needs cross-process coordination and the
version without it leaves a sweep that cannot reclaim, i.e. a cache that only grows. Two further
alternatives are recorded and rejected: per-database with no adoption (the stranding the task's own
risk line warns about), and keying the cache by anything other than the database.

**First-to-launch adoption is recorded as a choice**, in the decision and in
`adopt_legacy_cache`'s docstring, so a later reader does not reconstruct it as a consequence of
`ARC-006`.

**`core/paths.py` and `ai/TASKS.md` now point at `DAT-007`**, and both say plainly that they
previously called the boundary *"the one `ARC-006` already drew"*.

## §1 · `T180-R2` — the seam is inside the gate now

**What you found:** `test_a_sweep_cannot_reach_another_databases_pictures` derives both roots
itself, writes both files by hand, and builds one `QueueView`/`ThumbnailStore`. It proves
`cache_root_for` separates roots handed to it, and stays green if composition stops deriving one or
`MainWindow` stops passing it on.

**Two new regressions in `tests/integration/test_composition.py`**, both through the `composed`
fixture — real `application.compose`, real windows:

- `test_two_databases_get_two_thumbnail_caches_through_composition` — two whole applications over
  two databases sharing one cache parent. Files are written through **each composition's own
  resolved root**, and the sweep is driven on the store **that composition's queue view actually
  holds**. Nothing in it names `cache_root_for`.
- `test_the_window_hands_the_partitioned_root_to_both_of_its_stores` — the queue's store and the add
  dialog's must share one root, because `cache_generation` is keyed by directory and two roots put
  `T118-R16`'s count back out of reach. A window that partitioned only the queue would pass the
  first test and still leave the dialog writing into the directory this task exists to empty.

**Mutation-checked where you asked — at the seam, not in the helper:**

|---|---|
| `compose` stops deriving the root from the database | **caught** |
| `MainWindow` stops passing `cache_root` to the queue store | **caught** (both cases) |

The old test survives all three. It is **kept**, as the narrower unit claim, with a docstring that
now says so — a unit claim and a wiring claim fail for different reasons, and a suite holding only

### Two small production changes this required, and why they are not test-only seams

- **`compose(cache_directory=...)`** — the same injection `settings_file` and `geometry_file`
  already exist for, and for the same stated reason: without it a test of this seam writes into the
  developer's real cache, which `ai/TESTING.md` §5 forbids. The finding is precisely that this seam
  had no test.
- **`Composition.cache_root`** and **`ThumbnailStore.cache_root`** — held and exposed read-only for
  `database_path`'s reason: the partition is a claim about the assembled graph, and a root nothing
  can reach is a root nothing can check.
